Frequently Asked Questions

Do the QR codes created on this website expire?

No. All QR codes generated here are permanent static QR codes containing the direct encoded payload. They do not route through intermediary redirect servers, meaning they will work forever with zero expiration dates or monthly subscription fees.

Can I add my company or personal logo to the center of the QR code?

Yes. Simply upload your logo in the "Custom Logo (Optional)" field. The generator automatically applies Level H Error Correction (allowing up to 30% visual coverage) and renders your logo crisp in the center of the high-resolution canvas.

What formats can I encode with this QR tool?

You can generate QR codes for standard website URLs, Wi-Fi network credentials (WPA/WPA2/WEP), vCard business contacts, pre-filled email messages, and SMS dispatch.

Is my data secure and private when generating QR codes?

100% private. QR generation runs completely inside your browser client-side using JavaScript. Your text, Wi-Fi passwords, and contact info are never uploaded to our servers or saved in any database.
